   - 뜻/뉘앙스: 장소의 쓰임새를 나열할 때 `It's good for A, B, and C` 구조.

3. **"If you hear about something happening in Gangnam, it's usually quite big and quite expensive."**
   - 뜻/뉘앙스: 일반화해서 설명할 때 `If you hear about X, it's usually Y` — 지역/브랜드 이미지를 소개할 때 유용.

4. **"They really take this kind of advertising seriously."**
   - 뜻/뉘앙스: "진지하게 신경 쓴다"는 의미의 `take X seriously` — 기업/문화 특징 설명할 때 자주 씀.

5. **"Whether you're coming here in the daytime or at night, this area will always have something for you to do."**
   - 뜻/뉘앙스: `Whether A or B, X will always...` 구조로 "언제 오든 상관없이"를 표현.

6. **"It's supposed to bring you good luck in your relationships."**
   - 뜻/뉘앙스: 미신/속설을 설명할 때 `It's supposed to + 동사원형`.

7. **"You can come here quite quickly, get the work done you want, have something delicious, go home and rest, and then enjoy the nightlife."**
   - 뜻/뉘앙스: 동선을 순서대로 나열하는 구조 — 여행/코스 설명 영상에 그대로 응용 가능.

8. **"I feel like something clicked."**
   - 뜻/뉘앙스: "뭔가 딱 맞아떨어지는/전환되는 느낌이었다"는 관용 표현. 관계나 상황이 갑자기 달라졌을 때 씀.

9. **"It was too perfect. So, something must be wrong."**
