# 2026-08-25 — daily routine & session logistics
**Focus**: talking about today's events (morning routine, gym, work)

## Fixes
| I said | Natural version | Why |
|---|---|---|
| after eatting lunch my mother bring to my company by car | after lunch, my mother brought me to work | narrating the past needs past tense, not present |
| so i astonished | it startled me a bit | "astonish" needs a different subject — you can't say "I astonished" to mean "I was surprised" |
| i will really practice tomorrow as many as i can | I'll really practice as much as I can tomorrow | "practice" is uncountable — pairs with "much," not "many" |
| i want you make all git file | I want you to make the files | after "want someone," you need "to + verb" |
| i can communicate succeeded comunications what we talk about | I can look back on what we talked about | original was a jumble of nouns rather than a clear sentence |

## Expressions worth stealing
- **creature of habit** — 규칙적으로 똑같이 사는 사람 · used as: "You're clearly a creature of habit — leg day, pull-ups, protein with every meal, nothing throws you off for long."
- **slammed** — (일이) 엄청 바쁜 · used as: "What's actually got you slammed at the office right now?"
- **caught off guard** — 허를 찔리다/당황하다 · suggested as alternative to "astonished": "it caught me off guard"

## One thing to practice next time
- When telling a story about the past, keep checking each verb stays in past tense — don't let it slip back to present partway through (e.g. "bring" instead of "brought").
