## small talk
- **"You're a creature of habit."** — 규칙적으로 똑같이 반복하며 사는 사람
  - in context: "You're clearly a creature of habit — leg day, pull-ups, protein with every meal, nothing throws you off for long."
- **"What's got you slammed?"** — 뭐 때문에 그렇게 바빠?
  - in context: "What's actually got you slammed at the office right now, anything interesting or just the usual grind?"
- **"It caught me off guard."** — 허를 찔렸다 / 당황했다
  - in context: suggested as a natural alternative to "I astonished," for a sudden thunderstorm surprising you
  - my version was: "so i astonished" (ungrammatical — wrong verb form)
