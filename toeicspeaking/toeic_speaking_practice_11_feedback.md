## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken on a seashore, in the background i can see lots of trees. on the right side, i can see 2 people who seem to be holding trash bags and picing up some trash. i think they are cleaning the site. in the foreground, i can see a man who is smiling overall i think it looks like a typical calm scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken by a riverside. In the background, I can see lots of trees. On the right side, I can see two people who seem to be holding trash bags and picking up some trash — I think they are cleaning the area. In the foreground, I can see a man who is smiling and holding a big trash bag. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, cheerful cleanup scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `on a seashore` → `by a riverside`: 사진은 바다가 아니라 강가/호숫가에 가까운 배경이라 `seashore`(해안)보다 `riverside`가 더 정확합니다.
- 마지막 문장에 `and holding a big trash bag`를 추가했습니다 — 원래 답변엔 앞쪽 남성이 "웃고 있다"고만 하고, 정작 이 사진의 핵심 요소인 "큰 쓰레기봉투를 들고 있다"는 내용이 빠져 있었습니다.

### Q4

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken at a icecream store. in the background i can see some bottles which are arraged in a row. on the left side i can see a clerk who seems to be holiding a ice cream snack in a hands and using a tool in the other hand. in the middle of the picture i can see a variety of ice creams. i think some of them is strawberry flavor and one of them is chocolate falvor. overal i think it looks like a typical calm store scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken at an ice cream store. In the background, I can see some bottles arranged in a row. On the left side, I can see a clerk who seems to be holding an ice cream cone in one hand and using a tool in the other. In the middle of the picture, I can see a variety of ice cream. I think some of them are strawberry flavor, and one of them is chocolate flavor. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, calm store scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `at a icecream store` → `at an ice cream store`: 관사(a→an).
- `holding a ice cream snack in a hands` → `holding an ice cream cone in one hand`: 관사(a→an), `snack`보다 `cone`(콘)이 정확한 단어, "한쪽 손에"는 `in a hands`가 아니라 `in one hand` (Set 03에서 배운 `in one hand ... with the other`와 같은 패턴입니다).
- `some of them is` → `some of them are`: `them`(복수)에 맞춰 동사도 복수형.
- `typical calm store scene` → `a typical, calm store scene`: 관사 `a` 누락.

## Part 3. Respond to Questions

### Q5

**내 원문:**
> yes in my childhood, i had participated in a volunterr activity, and i did making a document in the office.

**교정 문장:**
> Yes, in my childhood, I participated in a volunteer activity, and I made some documents in the office.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `i had participated` → `I participated`: 특정 과거 시점의 단일 경험을 말할 땐 단순과거로 충분합니다 (`had participated`는 다른 과거 사건과 비교할 때 씀).
- `i did making a document` → `I made some documents`: `did + V-ing`라는 구조 자체가 없습니다 — 단순과거 `made`면 충분합니다.

### Q6

**내 원문:**
> I think the easiest way for a busy person to find time to volunteer is just download apps. there are lots of lists on the apps.

**교정 문장:**
> I think the easiest way for a busy person to find time to volunteer is to just download an app. There are lots of listings on these apps.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `is just download apps` → `is to just download an app`: `~하는 방법은 …이다`는 `the way ... is to + 동사원형` 구조라 `to`가 필요합니다.

### Q7

**내 원문:**
> I think it doesn't make sense. first, there a lots of education which the volunteers should learn. and they can make mistake, second as for advice i would tell a company to start with simple things such as let them make simple documents or copy some documents.

**교정 문장:**
> I think it doesn't make sense. First, there is a lot of training that volunteers need before they start, and they can make mistakes. Second, as for advice, I would tell a company to start with simple things, such as letting them make simple documents or copy some documents.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `there a lots of education` → `there is a lot of training`: be동사(`is`) 누락 + `education`은 셀 수 없는 명사라 `a lots of`가 아니라 `a lot of`, 그리고 문맥상 `training`(교육/훈련)이 더 자연스러운 단어.
- `they can make mistake` → `they can make mistakes`: 복수형 누락.
- `such as let them make` → `such as letting them make`: `such as` 뒤에는 동명사(`letting`)가 와야 합니다.
- 의견과 조언 두 파트 모두 답한 구조는 8세트 연속 잘 유지되고 있습니다.

## Part 4. Respond to Questions Using Information Provided

### Q8

**내 원문:**
> it will start at 9 and it will be held on heaundae beach

**교정 문장:**
> It will start at 9, and it will be held at Haeundae Beach.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `held on heaundae beach` → `held at Haeundae Beach`: 지명(특정 장소 이름) 앞에는 보통 `at`을 씁니다.

### Q9

**내 원문:**
> yes you are correct, it will runs from 9 to 11, on aug 16 on sun. and it is rated 4.9

**교정 문장:**
> Yes, you are correct. It will run from 9 to 11 on Sunday, August 16, and it is rated 4.9.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `it will runs` → `it will run`: 조동사(`will`) 뒤에는 동사원형만 와야 하는데 `-s`가 붙었습니다.
- `on aug 16 on sun` → `on Sunday, August 16`: `on`을 두 번 쓰지 않고 요일과 날짜를 한 번에 묶어서 표현.
- 날짜, 시간, 평점(rating) 세 가지를 다 정확히 답한 건 좋았습니다.

### Q10 — 계산은 정확했습니다, 대안만 다듬었습니다

**내 원문:**
> unfortunaltely, you will miss the first 2hours of the food bank sorting, so i would suggest volunteering hard during the time.

**교정 문장:**
> Unfortunately, you will miss the first 2 hours of Food Bank Sorting. I'd suggest joining for the last hour once your Beach Cleanup is over, or asking a friend there to fill you in on what you missed.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `i would suggest volunteering hard during the time` → 구체적인 대안으로 교체: "그 시간 동안 열심히 봉사하라"는 조언은 "일정이 겹친다"는 문제 자체를 해결해주지 않습니다. 이 유형의 질문은 "겹치는 상황을 어떻게 해결할지" 구체적인 대안(늦게 합류하기, 다른 사람에게 물어보기 등)을 제시하는 게 핵심입니다.
- 시간 계산(앞 2시간을 놓친다) 자체는 정확했습니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> I think online customer revicews are more trustworthy than officail advertising., there are some reasons that support my opinion, first, there are some exagrations on official advertising because their target is rasing money, second online reviews will be written whenever and wherever they are, all the reviwers have to do is just use smartphones. and they can upload the revicews with picture, it can also make more trustworthy. so that is why i agree with online customer reviews are more trustworhy than officail advertising.

**교정 문장:**
> I think online customer reviews are more trustworthy than official advertising. There are some reasons that support my opinion. First, there are some exaggerations in official advertising because their goal is to raise sales. Second, online reviews can be written whenever and wherever people want — all reviewers have to do is use their smartphones. They can also upload reviews with pictures, which makes them even more trustworthy. So that's why I agree that online customer reviews are more trustworthy than official advertising.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `exagrations on official advertising` → `exaggerations in official advertising`: 전치사는 `on`이 아니라 `in`.
- `their target is rasing money` → `their goal is to raise sales`: `is` 뒤에 목적을 말할 땐 동명사가 아니라 `to + 동사원형`이 와야 합니다 (`is raising` → `is to raise`).
- `whenever and wherever they are` → `whenever and wherever people want`: `they are`가 무엇을 가리키는지 불분명해서, 의도(원하는 시간/장소에 쓸 수 있다)를 살려 정리했습니다.
- `upload the revicews with picture` → `upload reviews with pictures`: 복수형 누락.
- `it can also make more trustworthy` → `which makes them even more trustworthy`: 목적어(`them`)가 빠져 있어서 채웠습니다.
- `i agree with online customer reviews are more trustworhy` → `I agree that online customer reviews are more trustworthy`: `agree with` 뒤에는 명사가 오고, 문장(절)을 이어받으려면 `agree that`을 써야 합니다 — Set 06에서도 짚었던 같은 패턴입니다.
- **참고로 잘한 점:** `all the reviwers have to do is just use smartphones`에서 `smartphones`를 복수형으로 써서 관사 문제 자체를 자연스럽게 피해가셨습니다. 이렇게 일반적인 진술에서 복수형을 쓰는 것도 좋은 전략입니다.

## 🎯 다음 세트 전 우선순위 TOP 3

1. **`agree with X` vs `agree that X`** — Set 06에서 배웠던 패턴인데 이번에 다시 헷갈렸습니다. `agree with`는 명사만, 문장 전체에 동의하려면 `agree that`이라는 걸 다시 한번 기억해두세요.
2. **Q10류 문제에서 "해결책"이 실제로 문제를 해결하는지 점검하기** — 이번엔 계산은 맞았지만 제안한 해결책이 상황과 안 맞았습니다.
3. **`such as` 뒤에는 동명사** — `such as let them` 같은 실수가 계속 나옵니다.

`is/are rated`와 시간 겹침 계산은 이제 확실히 안정된 것 같습니다. 다음 세트 필요하시면 말씀해주세요.
