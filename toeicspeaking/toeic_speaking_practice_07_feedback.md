## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in a library, in the background, a lot of books are on the selves in a row, in the middle of the picture, i can see many people who are reading a book or laptop, on the left side, i can see a man who is looking for a book. i think it's like a typical calm library picture.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a library. In the background, a lot of books are on the shelves in a row. In the middle of the picture, I can see many people who are reading a book or using a laptop. On the left side, I can see a man who is looking for a book. I think it's like a typical, calm library picture.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `reading a book or laptop` → `reading a book or using a laptop`: `read`는 책에는 맞지만 노트북에는 안 맞는 동사라서, 노트북 쪽엔 `using`을 따로 붙였습니다 (하나의 동사를 두 가지 다른 목적어에 억지로 걸치면 어색해집니다).

### Q4

**내 원문:**
> this picture was taken at a bakery store. in the background. i can see lots of kind of bread on the selves. and some fixutres hanging on the ceiling. and there is a woman who is wearing uniform. i think she seems to making some breads. i think it looks like a typical and calm bakery store picture.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a bakery. In the background, I can see lots of kinds of bread on the shelves, and some light fixtures hanging from the ceiling. There is a woman who is wearing a uniform. I think she seems to be arranging some bread. I think it looks like a typical, calm bakery scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `taken at a bakery store` → `taken in a bakery`: 실내 공간은 `in`이 맞고, `bakery` 자체가 이미 "빵집"이라는 뜻이라 `store`를 또 붙이면 중복입니다.
- `lots of kind of bread` → `lots of kinds of bread`: `kind`도 복수형이 필요합니다.
- `fixutres hanging on the ceiling` → `fixtures hanging from the ceiling`: "천장에 매달려 있다"는 `hang from the ceiling`입니다 — 이전 세트(02, 06)에서도 짚었던 표현인데 이번에 `on`으로 다시 나왔습니다.
- `who is wearing uniform` → `who is wearing a uniform`: 관사 `a` 누락.
- `she seems to making some breads` → `she seems to be arranging some bread`: `seems to` 뒤에는 `be + V-ing`가 와야 하고(`be` 누락), `bread`는 셀 수 없는 명사라 `breads`가 아니라 `bread`. 그리고 힌트에 나온 동작(진열대 정리)에 맞춰 `arranging`으로 바꿨습니다.

### Q7

**내 원문:**
> first i think this trend is make sense. I saw a lot of tablet ordering systems recently it is really easy to order. and i often saw robot food delivery. it is sometimes little bit depressed that it is so slow to deliver and sometimes it is unsafe. second as for adivce, i would tell the restaurant to make the system same as what the servant did, such as their delivery speed, and politeness and so on.

**교정 문장:**
> First, I think this trend makes sense. I've seen a lot of tablet ordering systems recently, and they're really easy to use. I've also often seen robot food delivery, but it's sometimes a little disappointing that it's so slow, and sometimes it feels unsafe. Second, as for advice, I would tell the restaurant to make the system as good as what servers used to provide, such as their delivery speed and politeness.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `this trend is make sense` → `this trend makes sense`: `is`와 `make`를 같이 쓰면 안 됩니다 — `makes sense` 하나로 충분합니다.
- `it is sometimes little bit depressed` → `it's sometimes a little disappointing`: `depressed`는 사람의 감정(우울한)을 나타내는 단어라 상황 묘사에는 안 맞습니다. 상황이 "실망스럽다"는 뜻이면 `disappointing`이 맞습니다.
- `what the servant did` → `what servers used to provide`: `servant`(하인)와 `server`(종업원)는 전혀 다른 단어입니다 — 철자가 비슷해서 헷갈리기 쉬우니 주의하세요.
- 의견과 조언 두 파트 모두 답한 구조는 이번에도 정확했습니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> i think adopting a four day work week is better than a five day work week. first if they work only 4 days a week. they get 1 more day off. it make them more relax, and they can make scedule for 4 days rather than 5days. in my case. my company start with 5days a week. and 2 years later, we changed our work shift to 4days. first time. we were hard to apapt the shift. but we still work hard and adapt easily later. after 1month everyone satisfied with the workshift and their productivity has grown up. so that is why i think 4days work is better than 5days work.

**교정 문장:**
> I think adopting a four-day work week is better than a five-day work week. First, if people work only four days a week, they get one more day off, which makes them more relaxed, and they can plan their schedule around four days rather than five. In my case, my company started with a five-day work week, and two years later, we changed our work shift to a four-day week. At first, it was hard for us to adapt to the new shift, but we worked hard and adapted more easily later on. After one month, everyone was satisfied with the new work shift, and our productivity had increased. So that's why I think a four-day work week is better than a five-day one.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `it make them more relax` → `which makes them more relaxed`: 동사 수일치(`make`→`makes`) + 형용사형(`relax`→`relaxed`).
- `my company start with 5days a week` → `my company started with a five-day work week`: 과거 이야기이므로 과거시제(`started`) 필요 + 관사 `a` 추가.
- `we were hard to apapt the shift` → `it was hard for us to adapt to the new shift`: "우리가 적응하기 힘들었다"는 `it was hard for us to`가 맞는 구조입니다 (`we were hard`는 "우리 자신이 딱딱하다"는 뜻이 되어버립니다).
- `we still work hard and adapt easily later` → `we worked hard and adapted more easily later on`: 과거 경험을 얘기하는 중이라 시제를 과거로 통일했습니다.
- `everyone satisfied` → `everyone was satisfied`: be동사(`was`) 누락.
- `their productivity has grown up` → `our productivity had increased`: `grow up`은 "(아이가) 자라다"라는 뜻이라 생산성 같은 수치에는 안 맞습니다. `increase`가 맞는 단어입니다.
- **내용 면에서는 이번 세트 최고 답변입니다:** 의견 → 이유 → 회사에서 실제로 겪은 5일제→4일제 전환 경험(초반의 어려움 → 적응 → 생산성 향상)까지 구체적인 스토리로 뒷받침한 구조가 AL 수준입니다. 시제만 과거로 통일하면 완벽합니다.
