## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in a animal hospictal. in the background there is a window closed. and i can see a doctor who seems to be checking a dog. and i can also see a dog who seems to be sitting and wating for checking out. overall i think it looks like a typical calm animal hospital.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in an animal hospital. In the background, I can see a doctor who seems to be examining a dog. I can also see a dog who seems to be standing and waiting to be examined. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, calm animal hospital scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `in a animal hospictal` → `in an animal hospital`: 관사(a→an).
- `there is a window closed` 문장은 삭제했습니다 — 사진에는 창문이 뚜렷하게 보이지 않아서, 확실히 보이는 요소 위주로 정리하는 게 안전합니다.
- `sitting and wating for checking out` → `standing and waiting to be examined`: 사진 속 개는 앉아있는 게 아니라 진료대 위에 **서 있고**, `checking out`은 "계산하고 나가다"라는 뜻이라 병원 진료 상황엔 안 맞습니다 — "진찰받기를 기다리는 중"이라는 뜻의 `waiting to be examined`로 교정했습니다.

### Q7 — 조언 파트가 질문과 살짝 다른 방향이었습니다

**내 원문:**
> I think banning pets to reduce noise and damage complaints make sense. first the pets are make noise and make smell. so i would tell a pet owner who is looking for a pet freiendly place to live to train hard and avoid to make smell or noise.

**교정 문장:**
> I think banning pets to reduce noise and damage complaints makes sense. First, pets make noise and create smells. As for advice, I would tell a pet owner who is looking for a pet-friendly place to check the building's pet policy carefully before signing a lease, and to look for places with good soundproofing or a ground-floor unit.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `make sense` → `makes sense`: 동명사구(`banning pets...`)는 단수 취급이라 동사에 `-s` 필요.
- `the pets are make noise and make smell` → `pets make noise and create smells`: `are make`라는 구조가 없어서, 두 동사를 자연스럽게 정리했습니다.
- **조언 내용을 바꿨습니다:** 질문은 "반려동물과 함께 살 수 있는 곳을 찾는 사람에게 줄 조언"을 물었는데, 원래 답변은 "반려동물을 잘 훈련시켜 소음/냄새를 줄이라"는 내용이라 초점이 조금 달랐습니다. "어디를 찾아야 하는지"에 초점을 맞춘 조언(반려동물 허용 정책 확인하기, 방음 좋은 곳/저층 찾기)으로 바꿨습니다.

### Q10 — 정답이었습니다

**내 원문:**
> i think green valley kennels fits you are looking for. it takes only 8min and it costs %50 and it allows below 35 kg.

**교정 문장:**
> I think Green Valley Kennels fits what you're looking for. It takes only 8 minutes, it costs $50, and it accepts dogs up to 35kg.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `fits you are looking for` → `fits what you're looking for`: `what`이 빠져서 문장이 미완성이었습니다 — Set 05에서 배웠던 `It fits everything you're looking for`와 같은 패턴입니다.
- `it allows below 35 kg` → `it accepts dogs up to 35kg`: "35kg 이하 허용"이라는 뜻을 좀 더 명확한 표현으로 정리했습니다.
- 조건 3개(거리, 가격, 체중 제한)를 모두 확인하고 정답을 고른 건 완벽했습니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> I think self checkout kiosks are more convenient than human cashiers. there are some reasons that support my opinion. first kiost is convient it makes sense. even though it reduce jobs, but service quality will go up, they can do other than service for kiosk. and clients can do more easily, all they have to do is just click and waiting for the food. so i agree that self checkout kisosk are more coveenient than human cashiers.

**교정 문장:**
> I think self-checkout kiosks are more convenient than human cashiers. There are some reasons that support my opinion. First, kiosks are convenient, so that makes sense on its own. Even though they reduce some jobs, service quality can actually improve, because employees can focus on other tasks instead of just handling checkout. Also, it's easier for customers — all they have to do is click and wait. So I agree that self-checkout kiosks are more convenient than human cashiers.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `even though it reduce jobs` → `even though they reduce some jobs`: 주어(kiosks, 복수)에 맞춰 `reduce`를 그대로 쓰되 주어를 `they`로 맞췄고, 단수 주어로 읽힐 경우엔 `reduces`가 필요합니다.
- `even though ~, but ~` → `even though ~, ~`: `even though`와 `but`을 같이 쓰면 대조 표현이 중복됩니다 — 하나만 쓰면 충분합니다.
- `they can do other than service for kiosk` → `employees can focus on other tasks instead of just handling checkout`: 원래 문장의 의도(직원들이 계산 대신 다른 업무를 할 수 있다)를 명확한 문장으로 재구성했습니다.
- `click and waiting` → `click and wait`: `is + 동사원형` 구조이므로 두 동사 모두 원형으로 맞춰야 합니다.
- `self checkout kisosk are` → `self-checkout kiosks are`: `kiosk`(단수)와 `are`(복수 동사)가 안 맞아서 `kiosks`로 복수형 처리했습니다.
