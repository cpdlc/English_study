## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in a room. in the background there is a white wall. i can see a woman who seems to be sitting and smiling with folding her arm. on the left side, i can see 3 baskets stacked in a pile. i also can see 2 items in glass box. also i can see 2 round box stacked overal i think it looks like a typical calm room scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a room. In the background, there is a white wall. I can see a woman who seems to be sitting and smiling, resting her chin on her hand. On the left side, I can see three baskets stacked in a pile. I can also see two items in glass boxes, and two round boxes stacked. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, calm room scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `smiling with folding her arm` → `smiling, resting her chin on her hand`: 실제 사진 속 동작은 팔짱을 낀 게 아니라 손으로 턱을 괴고 있는 자세입니다.
- `2 items in glass box` → `two items in glass boxes`: 복수형 누락.
- `2 round box stacked` → `two round boxes stacked`: 복수형 누락.

### Q4

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken at a cloth store. i can see lots of clothes some of them are striped shirts and some of them are funny painting shirts. on the left side i can see a woman who seems to checking the clothes, the clothes are arranged in a row. overall i think it looks like a typical calm cloth store.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken at a clothing store. I can see lots of clothes — some of them are striped shirts, and some of them have colorful patterns. On the left side, I can see a woman who seems to be looking through the clothes. The clothes are arranged in a row. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, calm clothing store.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `cloth store` → `clothing store`: `cloth`는 "옷감/천"이라는 재료를 가리키고, "옷 가게"는 `clothing store`가 맞습니다.
- `funny painting shirts` → `shirts with colorful patterns`: "재밌는 그림 셔츠"보다 사진에 더 잘 맞는 표현으로 정리했습니다.
- `seems to checking` → `seems to be looking through`: `seems to` 뒤에는 `be + V-ing`가 필요한데 `be`가 빠졌었습니다 — Set 07에서도 같은 패턴이 있었습니다.

### Q6

**내 원문:**
> i think the biggist reason people struggle to keep their homes organized is to make their mind, if their home is messy, they should not do anything and be lazy.

**교정 문장:**
> I think the biggest reason people struggle to keep their homes organized is their mindset. If their home is messy, they don't feel like doing anything, and they become lazy.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `is to make their mind` → `is their mindset`: "마음을 만들다"라는 표현이 의도한 뜻(마음가짐/의지 부족)과 잘 안 맞아서, "마음가짐"이라는 명사로 바로 정리했습니다.
- `they should not do anything and be lazy` → `they don't feel like doing anything, and they become lazy`: 원문은 "그들은 아무것도 하지 말고 게을러야 한다"는 **권유**처럼 읽히는데, 의도하신 건 "집이 지저분하면 (자연스럽게) 아무것도 하기 싫어지고 게을러진다"는 **결과 설명**이었을 거라 그에 맞게 정리했습니다.

### Q7

**내 원문:**
> i think it depends on the situation. if i were a rich, i will hire professional organizers, but if not i will do it myself, second as for advice, i would tell someone who wants to declutter on their own without hiring help to start with simple things such as cleaning and folding clothes and washing dishes.

**교정 문장:**
> I think it depends on the situation. If I were rich, I would hire professional organizers, but if not, I would do it myself. Second, as for advice, I would tell someone who wants to declutter on their own to start with simple things, such as cleaning, folding clothes, and washing dishes.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `if i were a rich` → `if I were rich`: `rich`는 형용사라서 앞에 `a`를 붙이지 않습니다 (`a rich person`이라면 몰라도 `rich` 단독으로는 명사처럼 못 씀).
- `i will hire` / `i will do it myself` → `I would hire` / `I would do it myself`: `if I were ~`(가정법 과거, 현실과 반대되는 상황을 가정)로 시작했으면 뒤에도 `will`이 아니라 `would`가 와야 짝이 맞습니다.
- 조언이 질문의 초점(혼자 정리하는 법)에 정확히 맞은 건 이번 세트에서 특히 좋았던 부분입니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> I think e books are better for learning than physical books. there are some reaons that support my opinion. first i can read wherever and whenever i want, all i have to do is just use a smartphone. when i go to work or friend, i can read, also i can memo it with using my smartphone, and i can screenshot, and it is more cheap than buying physical books, so i disagree that physical books are better for learning than e books.

**교정 문장:**
> I think e-books are better for learning than physical books. There are some reasons that support my opinion. First, I can read wherever and whenever I want — all I have to do is use a smartphone. When I'm commuting or meeting friends, I can read. I can also take notes using my smartphone, and I can take screenshots. It's also cheaper than buying physical books. So I disagree that physical books are better for learning than e-books.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `when i go to work or friend` → `when I'm commuting or meeting friends`: "일하러 가거나 친구"라는 문장이 미완성이라, 의도(출퇴근길/친구 만나러 갈 때)에 맞게 정리했습니다.
- `i can memo it with using my smartphone` → `I can take notes using my smartphone`: `memo`는 영어에서 동사로 잘 안 쓰고("메모하다"는 `take notes`), `with using`도 중복이라 `using` 하나로 정리했습니다.
- `it is more cheap` → `it's cheaper`: `cheap`처럼 짧은 형용사는 `more`가 아니라 `-er`을 붙여 비교급을 만듭니다.
- `i disagree that physical books are better` → 이 구조는 정확했습니다 — `agree/disagree that + 절` 패턴을 잘 적용하셨습니다.
