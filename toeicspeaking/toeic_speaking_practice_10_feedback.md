## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in a book store. i can see a woman who seems to be taking a book. in the background. i can see a lot of books are arranged on shelves. in the middle of the picture. i can see some books are stacked on some books. in the fore ground i also can see some piles. i think it looks like a typical and calm book store scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a bookstore. I can see a woman who seems to be taking a photo of the bookshelf with her phone. In the background, I can see a lot of books arranged on shelves. In the middle of the picture, I can see a stack of books on a table. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, calm bookstore scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `taking a book` → `taking a photo of the bookshelf with her phone`: 이게 이번 답변에서 가장 중요한 수정입니다 — 여성은 책을 손에 들고 있는 게 아니라, 휴대폰으로 책장 사진을 찍고 있습니다. 동작을 다시 한번 자세히 관찰하는 습관이 필요해 보입니다.
- `some books are stacked on some books` → `a stack of books on a table`: "책들이 책들 위에 쌓여있다"는 반복적이고 헷갈리는 표현이라, 테이블 위 책 더미를 가리키는 명확한 표현으로 정리했습니다.
- `a lot of books are arranged on shelves`는 정확했습니다 — `arranged` 표현을 계속 잘 쓰고 계십니다.

### Q4

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in a bar, in the background i can see lots of kinds of bodcars and alchols. and i also can see a man who seems to be a bartendar. he seems to be making a cocktail. in the foreground i can see some cups which are stacked on a shelf. overal i think it looks like a typical calm bar scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a bar. In the background, I can see lots of bottles of liquor. I can also see a man who seems to be a bartender — he seems to be making a cocktail. In the foreground, I can see some metal cups on the counter. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, calm bar scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `lots of kinds of bodcars and alchols` → `lots of bottles of liquor`: "bodcars"가 어떤 단어를 의도하신 건지 알아보기 어려워서(아마 bottles였을까요?), 사진에 맞는 표현으로 정리했습니다.
- `some cups which are stacked on a shelf` → `some metal cups on the counter`: 실제로는 선반이 아니라 바 카운터 위에 금속 컵(셰이커)들이 나란히 놓여 있는 모습이라, 위치를 더 정확하게 고쳤습니다.
- **참고:** 이번 사진엔 천장에 매달린 펜던트 조명이 있어서 `hanging from the ceiling`을 연습해볼 기회였는데, 이번 답변에는 조명 언급이 없었습니다. 다음에 조명이 있는 사진이 나오면 한번 써보세요.

### Q6

**내 원문:**
> I think the biggisest reason people use their phones right before going to sleep is to not thinking about the routine idea such as their work or relationships and so on.

**교정 문장:**
> I think the biggest reason people use their phones right before going to sleep is to avoid thinking about daily stress, such as their work or relationships.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `to not thinking about` → `to avoid thinking about`: `to + not + V-ing`라는 구조 자체가 없습니다. "~하는 걸 피하다"는 `avoid + V-ing`가 정확한 표현입니다.
- `the routine idea` → `daily stress`: "일상적인 생각"보다 "일상적인 스트레스"가 문맥(일/인간관계 걱정)에 더 정확합니다.

### Q7

**내 원문:**
> I think it makes sense that banning smartphones during class hours. first Using smartphone makes class messy. second students may not focus on classes while they use smartphones. as for advice, i would tell a school to start with simple things such as make policy.

**교정 문장:**
> I think it makes sense to ban smartphones during class hours. First, using a smartphone makes the class messy. Second, students may not focus on class while they use their smartphones. As for advice, I would tell a school to start with simple things, such as making a clear policy.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `it makes sense that banning smartphones` → `it makes sense to ban smartphones`: `that` 뒤에는 완전한 절이 와야 하는데 동명사만 있어서 문법이 안 맞았습니다. `to + 동사원형`으로 바꾸는 게 훨씬 간단합니다.
- `Using smartphone makes class messy` → `using a smartphone makes the class messy`: 관사 `a`가 또 빠졌습니다 — `use a smartphone`은 여러 세트에 걸쳐 계속 나오는 실수라, 이 표현만큼은 의식적으로 신경 써보면 좋겠습니다.
- `such as make policy` → `such as making a clear policy`: `such as` 뒤에는 동명사(`making`)가 오고, 관사(`a`)도 필요합니다.
- 의견과 조언 두 파트 모두 답한 구조는 7세트 연속 잘 유지되고 있습니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> I think shchools should ban smartphones during class to help students focus, there are some reasons that support my opinion. frist, there are lots of messages while they are using smartphones, so they have to focus on replying. and it cannot help focusing on class, i think student must focus on class first, and there a lots of things to do with smartphones, but in the school all they have to do is listen class. so that is why i think banning smartphones make sense.

**교정 문장:**
> I think schools should ban smartphones during class to help students focus. There are some reasons that support my opinion. First, there are lots of messages while they are using smartphones, so they end up focusing on replying instead of the lesson. Second, students must focus on class first, and there are a lot of things to do on smartphones that can distract them — but in school, all they have to do is listen to the class. So that's why I think banning smartphones makes sense.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `there a lots of things` → `there are a lot of things`: be동사(`are`) 누락.
- `listen class` → `listen to class`: `listen`은 뒤에 전치사 `to`가 필요합니다 (`listen to music`, `listen to the teacher`처럼).
- `banning smartphones make sense` → `banning smartphones makes sense`: 동명사(`banning smartphones`)는 단수 취급이라 동사에 `-s`가 필요합니다.
- **논리 구조는 이번에 좋았습니다** — "메시지 확인에 정신이 팔린다 → 수업에 집중 못 한다 → 스마트폰엔 딴짓거리가 많다"로 한 방향을 계속 유지했습니다. Set 08~09에서 살짝 흔들렸던 논리 일관성이 이번엔 다시 탄탄했습니다.

## 🎯 다음 세트 전 우선순위 TOP 3

1. **`use a smartphone`** — 벌써 여러 세트에 걸쳐 반복되는 유일한 실수입니다. 이 표현 하나는 따로 소리 내어 여러 번 연습해보시는 걸 추천합니다.
2. **Q9 유형처럼 짧은 질문도 두 부분 다 답하기** — 이번엔 시간을 빠뜨렸습니다. Q7뿐 아니라 다른 문항에서도 "질문이 몇 개를 물었는지" 세어보는 습관이 필요합니다.
3. `there is/are` 존재문 be동사는 여전히 가끔 빠집니다 — 계속 체크해주세요.

`will be + p.p.`와 `is/are rated`는 이번에 확실히 잡힌 것 같습니다 — 다음 세트에서도 유지되는지 지켜보겠습니다. 다음 세트 필요하시면 말씀해주세요.
