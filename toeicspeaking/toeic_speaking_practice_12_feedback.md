## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in an office, in the background i can see some items displayed on a shelf. in the foreground i can see a man who seems to be playing guitar and sitting on a couch. i also can see a laptop on the couch. overall i think it looks like a typical calm funny scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a living room. In the background, I can see some items displayed on a shelf. In the foreground, I can see a man who seems to be sitting on a couch and playing guitar. I can also see a laptop on the couch. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, relaxed scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `taken in an office` → `taken in a living room`: 소파, 벽화, 장식 선반이 있는 가정집 거실 장면이라 사무실(office)이 아닙니다.
- `typical calm funny scene` → `typical, relaxed scene`: `calm`과 `funny`는 서로 결이 다른 형용사를 나열해서 어색합니다 — 하나의 통일된 분위기로 정리했습니다.

### Q4

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in a work office. in the backgorund i can see some pots arranged on shelves. and some items are stacked on the shelves. in the foreground i can also see some pots which are made right before. in the middle of the picture i can see a man who seeems to be making pot. he is focusing on making. overal i think it looks like a typical passionate scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a pottery workshop. In the background, I can see some pots arranged on shelves, and some items are stacked on the shelves. In the foreground, I can also see some pots that were just finished. In the middle of the picture, I can see a man who seems to be making a pot — he looks very focused on his work. Overall, I think it looks like a typical, passionate workshop scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `in a work office` → `in a pottery workshop`: Q3에 이어 이번에도 "office"라고 하셨는데, 물레와 도자기가 있는 도예 작업실입니다. 장소를 판단할 때 사진 속 도구/사물을 먼저 살펴보면 좋겠습니다.
- `some pots which are made right before` → `some pots that were just finished`: "만들어지기 직전"이라는 어색한 표현을, "방금 완성된"이라는 자연스러운 표현으로 정리했습니다.
- `seems to be making pot` → `seems to be making a pot`: 관사 `a` 누락.
- `he is focusing on making` → `he looks very focused on his work`: `making` 뒤에 목적어가 없어서 문장이 미완성이었습니다.

## Part 3. Respond to Questions

### Q5

**내 원문:**
> I tried to learn playing guitar. it was little bit hard but it makes me laugh.

**교정 문장:**
> I tried to learn to play guitar. It was a little bit hard, but it made me laugh.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `learn playing guitar` → `learn to play guitar`: `learn`은 뒤에 동명사가 아니라 `to + 동사원형`을 씁니다.
- `little bit hard` → `a little bit hard`: 관사 `a` 누락 — 이전에 배우신 `a little bit disappointing`과 같은 패턴입니다.
- `it makes me laugh` → `it made me laugh`: 과거의 특정 경험을 얘기하는 중이라 과거형이 자연스럽습니다.

**내 원문:**
> I think online video tutorials are as effective as taking a class with an instructor. first it can be controled by the users. all they have to do is just click the button so they can stop or play whenever they want. second as for advice. i would tell someone who wants to learn a skill to repeat again and again untill getting skills.

**교정 문장:**

## Part 4. Respond to Questions Using Information Provided

### Q8

**내 원문:**
> it costs $450 and it include insurance.

**교정 문장:**
> It costs $450, and it includes insurance.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `it include insurance` → `it includes insurance`: 3인칭 단수 주어(it)에는 동사에 `-s`가 필요합니다.

### Q9 — 요일을 잘못 읽으셨습니다

**내 원문:**
> yes you are right, it is rated 4.9, and their move is on sat sep 7, and they will run from 9 to 12.

표에는 SafePack Movers가 **Sun(일요일), Sep 7**로 되어 있는데, 답변에서 **Sat(토요일)**로 잘못 말씀하셨습니다.

**교정 문장:**
> Yes, you are right. It is rated 4.9, their move is on Sunday, September 7, and they will run from 9 to 12.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `sat sep 7` → `Sunday, September 7`: 표를 다시 확인해보면 이 업체의 날짜는 일요일입니다. 숫자(9월 7일) 자체는 맞았지만 요일을 헷갈리셨습니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> i think rasising the minimum wage doesn't helps improve workers' quality of life. job losses and higher prices make sense. for example if the minimum wage raise, owner will work instead of hiring part time job, and i don't know why exactly the prices are going higher if the minimum wage raise. and the economy get worse. so that is why i agree with avoiding raising the minimum wage

**교정 문장:**
> I think raising the minimum wage doesn't help improve workers' quality of life. Job losses and higher prices make sense to me. For example, if the minimum wage rises, owners will do the work themselves instead of hiring part-time workers. Prices also tend to go up, probably because businesses pass the extra labor cost on to customers. And the economy gets worse overall. So that's why I agree with avoiding a minimum wage increase.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `doesn't helps` → `doesn't help`: `doesn't`가 이미 부정과 3인칭 단수를 나타내므로, 뒤에 오는 동사는 원형(`help`)이어야 합니다.
- `if the minimum wage raise` → `if the minimum wage rises`: `raise`는 보통 "~을 올리다"라는 타동사라 이 자리(주어가 임금 자체)엔 `rise`(오르다, 자동사)가 더 정확합니다. 답변에 이 실수가 두 번 나왔습니다.
- `hiring part time job` → `hiring part-time workers`: "일자리"가 아니라 "사람"을 고용하는 것이므로 `job`이 아니라 `workers`.
- `the economy get worse` → `the economy gets worse`: 3인칭 단수 동사에 `-s` 누락.
- **내용 팁:** `i don't know why exactly the prices are going higher`처럼 "왜 그런지 모르겠다"고 인정하는 부분은, 의견을 주장하는 답변에서는 설득력을 약하게 만듭니다. 확실하지 않아도 그럴듯한 이유(예: "기업이 인건비 상승분을 가격에 전가하기 때문에")를 붙이는 게 훨씬 설득력 있는 답변이 됩니다 — 교정 문장에 그 이유를 채워 넣었습니다.
- 논리 구조 자체(임금 인상 → 고용 축소 → 가격 상승 → 경기 악화)는 한 방향으로 일관되게 잘 전개됐습니다.

3. **주어-동사 수일치 (`doesn't helps`, `economy get`)** — 여전히 가끔 나오는 기본적인 실수입니다.
