## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> this picture is taken in a market, i can see a woman who is wearing striped t shirts and pulling a kart, in the kart there are some snacks, in the background there are lots of snacks and food on the shelves, and some lights are hanging on the top, i think it is a typical calm market picture.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a market. I can see a woman who is wearing a striped t-shirt and pushing a cart. In the cart, there are some snacks. In the background, there are lots of snacks and food on the shelves, and some lights are hanging from the ceiling. I think it's a typical, calm market picture.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `this picture is taken` → `this picture was taken`: 사진이 찍힌 건 과거의 특정 순간이므로 과거시제(`was`)가 맞습니다. (Q4에서는 `was taken`을 정확히 쓰셨는데, Q3에서만 `is`로 흔들렸습니다.)
- `wearing striped t shirts` → `wearing a striped t-shirt`: 한 사람이 입은 셔츠는 하나이므로 단수 + 관사(`a`).
- `pulling a kart` → `pushing a cart`: 사진 속 동작은 카트를 미는(push) 것이지 당기는(pull) 게 아닙니다.
- `lights are hanging on the top` → `lights are hanging from the ceiling`: "천장에 매달려 있다"는 `hang from the ceiling`이 정확한 표현입니다.

### Q4

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken at a classroom. on the left side, i can see a man, who is wearing blue jeans and pointing somewhere and making gesture, on the right side, i can see 5 people, they are looking at somewhere man is pointing, i think this picture is typical warm classroom picture

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a classroom. On the left side, I can see a man who is pointing somewhere and making a gesture. On the right side, I can see five people who are looking at where the man is pointing. I think this is a typical, warm classroom picture.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `taken at a classroom` → `taken in a classroom`: 교실처럼 안이 있는 공간은 `in`을 씁니다 (`at`은 지점을 가리킬 때).
- `making gesture` → `making a gesture`: 관사 `a` 누락.
- `looking at somewhere man is pointing` → `looking at where the man is pointing`: `somewhere` 뒤에 바로 절이 올 수 없습니다 — "~하는 곳"을 나타낼 땐 `where + 주어 + 동사`를 씁니다.
- `this picture is typical warm classroom picture` → `this is a typical, warm classroom picture`: 관사 `a` 누락.

## Part 3. Respond to Questions

### Q5

**내 원문:**
> maybe i spend watching streaming services twice a week, i usually watch physical 100, it is really run.

**교정 문장:**
> I spend about five hours a week watching streaming services, and I usually watch Physical: 100 — it's really fun.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `spend watching streaming services twice a week` → `spend about five hours a week watching streaming services`: 질문이 "몇 시간(hours)"을 물었는데 "일주일에 두 번"(빈도)으로 답하셨습니다. `spend + 시간 + V-ing`는 원래 "시간"이 들어가는 자리라, 빈도(twice a week)가 아니라 시간(five hours a week)이 와야 질문에도 맞고 문법도 맞습니다.

### Q6

**내 원문:**
> there are 2 advantage of streaming services compared to traditional tv, first, i can watch it wherever i want, all i have to do is just use smartphone, and last i can watch it anytime even though i miss the time.

**교정 문장:**
> There are two advantages of streaming services compared to traditional TV. First, I can watch it wherever I want — all I have to do is use a smartphone. And second, I can watch it anytime, even if I miss the original airtime.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `2 advantage` → `two advantages`: 복수형 누락.
- `use smartphone` → `use a smartphone`: 관사 `a` 누락 — Set 02 Q6에서도 나왔던 정확히 같은 실수입니다.
- `even though i miss the time` → `even if I miss the original airtime`: "방송 시간을 놓쳐도"라는 의도를 더 명확한 단어로 표현했습니다.

### Q7

**내 원문:**
> absoultely, there are some struggling to compete with streaming plaforms. recently, majority of people use more streaming services rather than tv, because it is really easy to use. i would tell the tv network to make subscription and make more fans.

**교정 문장:**
> Absolutely, some TV networks are struggling to compete with streaming platforms. Recently, the majority of people use streaming services more than TV, because it's really easy to use. As for advice, I would tell the TV network to offer subscription plans and attract more fans.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `there are some struggling` → `some TV networks are struggling`: `some` 뒤에 무엇이 struggling한 건지 명사가 빠져서 채워 넣었습니다.
- `majority of people` → `the majority of people`: 관사 `the` 누락 — Set 03 Q7의 "majority of company"와 같은 패턴입니다.
- `make subscription` / `make more fans` → `offer subscription plans` / `attract more fans`: `make`보다 `offer`(서비스를 제공하다), `attract`(끌어모으다)가 훨씬 자연스러운 콜로케이션입니다.
- 의견과 조언 두 파트 모두 답한 구조는 정확했습니다.

### Q9

**내 원문:**
> it will arrive at 16 20 and it has no stop.

**교정 문장:**
> It will arrive at 16:20, and it has no stops.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `has no stop` → `has no stops`: `no` 뒤에 오는 셀 수 있는 명사는 복수형이 맞습니다 (`no stops`, `no problems`처럼).

### Q10 — 정답이었습니다

**내 원문:**
> you should boot flgith sk102, it will arrive at 9 30 and it costs only 420 and there is no stops. it fits what you want.

**교정 문장:**
> You should book flight SK102. It will arrive at 9:30, and it costs only $420, and there are no stops. It fits everything you want.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `there is no stops` → `there are no stops`: `stops`가 복수형이라 `is`가 아니라 `are`를 씁니다 — Q9의 실수와 같은 종류(단복수 일치)입니다.
- 조건 3개(가격, 시간, 경유)를 모두 정확히 확인하고 골라낸 건 완벽했습니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> I think social media does not more harm than good to society. first i can use social media wherever i want, all i have to do is just use smartphone. and i can know other people's life easily. in my case. i have a friend who live far from my house. i can know my friend's life such as his traveling, his studying and so on. finally there is a function which can communicate each other easily. so that is why i aree with the social media is better

**교정 문장:**
> I don't think social media does more harm than good to society. First, I can use social media wherever I want — all I have to do is use a smartphone. And I can keep up with other people's lives easily. In my case, I have a friend who lives far from my house, and I can keep up with his life, such as his travels and his studies. Finally, social media has a function that lets people communicate with each other easily. So that's why I agree that social media is more beneficial than harmful.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `does not more harm than good` → `doesn't do more harm than good`: 동사 `do`가 빠져서 문장이 성립하지 않았습니다.
- `use smartphone` → `use a smartphone`: 관사 `a` 누락 — 이번 세트에서만 Q6에 이어 두 번째로 나온 같은 실수입니다.
- `i can know other people's life` / `i can know my friend's life` → `keep up with other people's lives` / `keep up with his life`: "누군가의 삶을 안다"는 `know`보다 SNS로 근황을 계속 확인한다는 뜻의 `keep up with`가 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.
- `who live far from my house` → `who lives far from my house`: 3인칭 단수 주어(친구 한 명)엔 동사에 `-s`가 필요합니다.
- `communicate each other` → `communicate with each other`: `communicate`는 `with` 없이 "서로"를 바로 목적어처럼 쓸 수 없습니다.
- `i aree with the social media is better` → `I agree that social media is more beneficial than harmful`: `agree with` 뒤에는 명사/대명사가 오고, 문장(절)을 이어받으려면 `agree that + 절`을 써야 합니다.

## 🎯 다음 세트 전 우선순위 TOP 3

1. **`use a smartphone`** — 이번 세트에서만 두 번(Q6, Q11), 이전 세트까지 합치면 세 번째입니다. "smartphone 앞엔 항상 a"로 확실히 기억해두세요.
2. **`there is/are` + 명사의 단복수 일치** — Q9, Q10에서 `no stop(s)` 관련해서 계속 흔들렸습니다. 뒤에 오는 명사가 복수면 `are`, 단수/셀 수 없으면 `is`입니다.
3. **`communicate with each other`** — 새로 나온 실수인데, "서로 소통하다"는 항상 `with`가 필요하다는 걸 기억해두세요.
