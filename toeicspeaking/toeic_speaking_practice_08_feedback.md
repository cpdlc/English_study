## Part 2. Describe a Picture

### Q3

**내 원문:**
> This picture was taken in a building. I can see a man who is walking with holding a rope. and i can see a dog who pulling his boss. in the background. i can see lots of tiles in a row. on the left side there is a pole. overall it looks like a typical and calm scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken in a building. I can see a man who is walking while holding a leash. I can also see a dog who is pulling his owner. In the background, I can see lots of tiles in a row. On the left side, there is a pole. Overall, it looks like a typical, calm scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `walking with holding a rope` → `walking while holding a leash`: `with holding`이라는 구조 자체가 없고, `while + V-ing`가 "~하면서"에 맞는 구조입니다. 그리고 개를 데리고 다닐 때 쓰는 줄은 `rope`(밧줄)이 아니라 `leash`(목줄/리드줄)입니다.
- `a dog who pulling his boss` → `a dog who is pulling his owner`: `who` 뒤에 `is`가 빠졌고, 반려동물의 주인은 `boss`(직장 상사)가 아니라 `owner`입니다 — 완전히 다른 단어라 주의하세요.

### Q4

**내 원문:**
> Thick picture was taken at a park. i can see a man who seems to be sitting and watching something. and there are some fruits arranged on a table. in the background. i can see some trees in the foreground. i can see some grass. overall it looks like a typical calm park scene.

**교정 문장:**
> This picture was taken at an outdoor market. I can see a man who seems to be leaning over the table and arranging some fruit. There are some fruits arranged on a table. In the background, I can see some trees. Overall, it looks like a typical, calm market scene.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `taken at a park` → `taken at an outdoor market`: 나무들 사이에 있긴 하지만 과일을 파는 가판대라서, 공원보다는 "야외 시장/가판대"가 사진 내용에 더 정확합니다.
- `sitting and watching something` → `leaning over the table and arranging some fruit`: 사진 속 남자는 앉아서 무언가를 구경하는 게 아니라, 테이블 위로 몸을 숙여 과일을 정리하는 동작을 하고 있습니다.
- `in the background ... in the foreground` → `in the background`로 통일: 같은 나무를 두고 "배경"과 "전경"을 둘 다 썼는데, 서로 반대되는 위치라 앞뒤가 안 맞습니다. 하나로 정리했습니다.

### Q6

> because i commute to work by bicycle. so the most stressful part of commuting is weather.

**교정 문장:**

### Q7

**내 원문:**
> first . it is good idae to choose flexible commuting hours, but i think even if there is a flexible time, it has to be within 2hours, because some teams have to meeting, and other team have to communicate with other team. second as for advice, i would tell the company to make a attendance time to commute on time, if there is no timetable, the employee will commute whenever they want. it makes the company mess.

**교정 문장:**
> First, it is a good idea to allow flexible commuting hours, but I think even with flexible time, it has to stay within a 2-hour range, because some teams have to meet, and other teams have to communicate with each other. Second, as for advice, I would tell the company to set fixed core hours so employees still commute around the same time. If there is no timetable at all, employees will commute whenever they want, and that could make the company messy.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `good idae to choose` → `a good idea to allow`: 관사 `a` 누락.
- `have to meeting` → `have to meet`: `have to` 뒤에는 동사원형이 와야 합니다 (`meeting`은 -ing형이라 틀림).
- `other team have to communicate with other team` → `other teams have to communicate with each other`: `team`이 일반적인 진술이라 복수형(`teams`)이 필요하고, "다른 팀"을 두 번 반복하는 대신 `each other`로 정리.
- `make a attendance time` → `set fixed core hours`: 관사(`a`→`an`)도 있지만, "출근 시간을 만들다"는 의도를 더 명확한 표현(`core hours` — 회사에서 흔히 쓰는 "필수 근무 시간대" 개념)으로 바꿨습니다.
- `the employee will commute` → `employees will commute`: 일반적인 진술이라 복수형이 필요합니다.
- `it makes the company mess` → `that could make the company messy`: `mess`는 명사라 `make + 목적어 + mess`로 쓰려면 `a mess`가 필요하고, 형용사 `messy`를 쓰는 게 더 자연스럽습니다.
- 의견과 조언 두 파트 모두 답한 구조는 이번에도 정확했습니다.

### Q9

**내 원문:**
> you are correct, photographer c isn't available on sat. they have 6 years of experience. and it rated 4.8

**교정 문장:**
> You are correct, Photographer C isn't available on Saturday. They have 6 years of experience, and they are rated 4.8.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `it rated 4.8` → `they are rated 4.8`: be동사(`are`) 누락 — Set 05 Q9에서도 "it rated 4.6"으로 똑같이 틀렸던 부분입니다. 주어도 사람(사진작가)을 가리키므로 `it`보다 `they`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

### Q10 — 정답이었습니다

**내 원문:**
> I think photoprapher E is right person for your manager. it costs $75 and they have 7 years of experience. and they are available on sat and it rated 4.7. I think it is good choice for you manager.

**교정 문장:**
> I think Photographer E is the right person for your manager. It costs $75, and they have 7 years of experience. They are also available on Saturday, and they are rated 4.7. I think it's a good choice for your manager.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `is right person` → `is the right person`: 관사 `the` 누락.
- `it rated 4.7` → `they are rated 4.7`: Q9와 똑같은 실수가 이 답변에서도 반복됐습니다 — 이번 세트에서 두 번 나온 걸 보면 확실히 짚고 넘어가야 할 패턴입니다.
- `it is good choice` → `it's a good choice`: 관사 `a` 누락.
- 조건 5개를 모두 정확히 확인하고 정답을 고른 건 완벽했습니다.

## Part 5. Express an Opinion

### Q11

**내 원문:**
> I agree that company should not be allowed to collect personal date to provicde more personalized services and ads. there are some reasons that support my opinion, as you said, it is a serious incasion of privacy. and first employee cannot memorize all the people's data, so they cannot service as well, and i think all services should be served same for whoever they are, second if their service is bad, they have to get some feedback and have to better than before. so this is why i agree with.

**교정 문장:**
> I agree that companies should not be allowed to collect personal data to provide more personalized services and ads. There are some reasons that support my opinion. As the question mentioned, it is a serious invasion of privacy. First, employees can't realistically manage everyone's individual data, so personalized service isn't always reliable anyway. Also, I think all customers should receive the same service regardless of who they are. Second, if a company's service is bad, they should get feedback and improve it — that should happen regardless of whether they collect personal data. So that's why I agree with this view.

**무엇이 바뀌었는지:**
- `company should not` → `companies should not`: 일반적인 진술이라 복수형이 필요합니다 (이번 세트에서 `team`, `employee`와 함께 세 번째로 나온 같은 패턴입니다).
- `employee cannot memorize` → `employees can't realistically manage`: 복수형 교정 + "기억하다"보다 "관리하다"가 데이터 처리 맥락에 더 맞는 단어.
- `they cannot service as well` → `personalized service isn't always reliable anyway`: `service`를 동사처럼 쓴 부분을 자연스러운 표현으로 정리.
- `all services should be served same for whoever they are` → `all customers should receive the same service regardless of who they are`: 어색한 동어반복(`services should be served`)을 정리.
- `have to better than before` → `should ... improve it`: 동사(`get better`/`improve`)가 빠져 있던 부분을 채웠습니다.
- `so this is why i agree with` → `so that's why I agree with this view`: `agree with` 뒤에 목적어가 빠져서 채웠습니다.
- **내용 면에서 참고할 점:** 이번 논거 중 "직원이 데이터를 다 기억 못 하니 서비스가 어차피 안 좋다"는 이유와, "서비스가 나쁘면 피드백 받고 개선해야 한다"는 마지막 이유가 서로 살짝 다른 방향을 가리킵니다 (하나는 "개인화가 어차피 안 된다", 다른 하나는 "개선은 해야 한다"). Set 07의 4일제 답변처럼 하나의 방향으로 쭉 밀고 나가는 논리 구조를 다음 세트에서 다시 한번 신경 써보면 좋겠습니다.
