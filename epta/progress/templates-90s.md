# 90초 자유응답 템플릿 — 빈칸만 채우면 되는 틀

> 20·30초 문항(복창+α, 판단·이유, intentions, 두 질문, 발신)은
> [`drills.md`](drills.md) 에 실패율 순으로 정리돼 있다.

EPTA 에서 **90초짜리 문항은 세 개**뿐이다. 매 세트 똑같은 자리에, 거의 똑같은 문구로 나온다.

| 문항 | 질문 | 성격 |
|---|---|---|
| **Part Ⅱ A Follow-up** | `In this situation, ... / In your role as a pilot, ... / Give an example.` | 3부 구조 |
| **Part Ⅱ C Q1** | `What happened to your aircraft (HL123)? Explain the nature of the incident.` | 사실 전달 |
| **Part Ⅱ C Q2** | `How do you think the incident was handled by the air traffic controller? Do you feel the situation could have been handled differently?` | 의견 제시 |

Q1·Q2 는 **4세트 전부 한 글자도 다르지 않았다.** 그래서 외울 가치가 있다.

**분량 기준** : 90초는 말로 **6~8문장, 120~150단어**다. 문장 하나가 15~20단어면 적당하다.
아래 뼈대는 전부 그 길이에 맞춰 놨다.

---

## 1. Task C Q1 — 사건 설명

> What happened to your aircraft (HL123)? Explain the nature of the incident.

### 뼈대 (7문장)

```
① During the ______ we ______ .                    ← 언제, 무슨 일이
② We ______ , but ______ .                          ← 우리가 한 조치, 그 결과
③ So we told the controller ______ and requested ______ .
④ He gave us ______ .                               ← 관제사가 준 것
⑤ He also offered ______ , but ______ .             ← 선택지가 있었다면
⑥ Then ______ .                                     ← 상황 변화(회복/변심)
⑦ In the end we ______ .                            ← 결말
```

### ① 비행 단계 — `During the ______`

| 단계 | 영어 | 그동안 나온 세트 |
|---|---|---|
| 지상 유도 | `taxi` | — |
| 이륙 활주 | `takeoff roll` | Ⅰ-B 보고 문항에 자주 |
| 이륙 후 상승 | `climb out` | EPTA_03 (엔진 화재) |
| 순항 | `cruise` | EPTA_06 (여압 상실) |
| 강하 | `descent` | EPTA_07 · 08 |
| 접근 | `approach` | EPTA_01 · 02 · 04 · 05 · SAMPLE_01 |

> **주의** — 접근이 아닌데 `approach phase` 라고 쓴 적이 두 번 있다(EPTA_03 은 상승 중,
> EPTA_06 은 순항 중). **Situation Update 의 첫 줄에 적힌 고도를 보고 고른다.**
> 통과고도까지 붙이면 더 좋다 : `During the descent, passing 15,000 feet, ...`

### ② 사건 이름 — 여기가 가장 많이 비는 칸이다

**EPTA_06·07 두 세트 연속으로 사건 이름이 한 번도 안 나왔다.**
`that warning sign`, `we got lots of instruments display` 처럼 쓰면 무슨 일이었는지 전달되지
않는다. **Part Ⅱ B 가 시작되면 메모 맨 위에 사건 이름부터 한 줄로 적어 둔다.**

| 사건 | 영어 | 세트 |
|---|---|---|
| 유압 계통 저압 | `we lost hydraulic system B` | EPTA_01 |
| 속도계 불일치 | `our airspeed indications became unreliable` | EPTA_02 |
| 엔진 화재 | `we had a fire on engine number one` | EPTA_03 |
| 플랩 고착 | `the flaps jammed at 15 and would not extend any further` | EPTA_04 |
| 기수 기어 이상 | `we got a red nose gear unsafe indication` | EPTA_05 |
| 여압 상실 | `we got a cabin altitude warning and lost pressurization` | EPTA_06 |
| 화물칸 연기 | `we got a cargo smoke warning for the aft hold` | EPTA_07 |
| 조류 충돌·엔진 진동 | `we flew through a flock of birds and engine number two started vibrating` | EPTA_08 |
| 계기 표시 상실 | `we lost a lot of our instrument displays` | SAMPLE_01 |

> **`lost` 를 빠뜨리지 말 것.** `we got lots of instruments display` 라고 쓰면
> "계기를 많이 얻었다"가 되어 **원인이 반대로** 전달된다. (SAMPLE_01 실제 사례)

### ③ 우리가 한 조치 — `We ______`

| 조치 | 영어 |
|---|---|
| 체크리스트 수행 | `we ran the checklist` |
| 기어 재순환 | `we recycled the gear` |
| 대체 전개 | `we extended the gear by the alternate system` |
| 소화병 방출 | `we discharged the fire bottles` |
| 엔진 정지 | `we shut the engine down` |
| 추력 감소 | `we reduced thrust on that engine` |
| 긴급 강하 | `we started an emergency descent` |
| 산소 마스크 | `we put the oxygen masks on` |
| 비상 선포 | `we declared a mayday` / `we declared a pan-pan` |

> **조치를 말해야 심각도가 전달된다.** 소화병을 방출했다는 것은 "약 60분을 벌었다"는 뜻이고,
> 긴급 강하를 한다는 것은 "다른 항공기를 치워 달라"는 뜻이다. EPTA_07 에서 소화병 방출을,
> EPTA_06 에서 `emergency descent` 를 빠뜨려 두 번 다 지적받았다.

### ④~⑤ 요청과 선택지

```
③ …and requested ______ .
      holding so we could work the checklist
      vectors for troubleshooting
      priority for landing
      a lower level
      the fire service standing by

④ He gave us ______ .
      holding at ______ , left/right turns
      priority
      a vector clear of the high ground
      descent at our discretion

⑤ He also offered ______ , but ______ .
      a diversion to ______        / it was 800 metres in fog with no ILS
      runway ______ instead        / it was only 2,500 metres and we needed 2,900
      a low pass for a visual check / …
```

### ⑥ 상황 변화 — 거의 매 세트 있다

```
Then the warning went out about three minutes after we discharged the bottles.
Then we got some of our instruments back.
Then the vibration dropped and the engine ran normally at reduced thrust.
Then the cabin altitude stopped climbing and the passengers came off oxygen.
Then the tower reported the nose gear looked down and in place.
```

### ⑦ 결말

```
In the end we landed at ______ off the ILS runway ______ , with the fire service following us.
In the end we continued to ______ and landed safely.
```

> **결말을 사실대로.** EPTA_07 에서 "회항했다"고 썼는데 실제로는 원래 목적지에 내렸다.

### ★ 완성 예 (EPTA_08 기준, 128단어)

> During the descent, passing 15,000 feet, we flew through a flock of birds and engine
> number two started vibrating heavily. We reduced thrust on that engine and the vibration
> settled, but we had to keep the speed at 230 knots or less. So we told the controller
> what had happened and requested priority for landing. He gave us 210 knots for spacing
> and a vector for the ILS. Then the vibration dropped further and the engine ran normally
> at reduced thrust. He also offered us runway 09 instead of 27, because the wind had
> backed and 09 now had a headwind. In the end we took runway 09 and landed off the ILS
> with the fire service standing by.

## 2. Task C Q2 — 관제사 평가

> How do you think the incident was handled by the air traffic controller? Do you feel the
> situation could have been handled differently? Make a comment from a pilot's point of view.

**질문이 두 개다.** ① 잘 처리했나 ② 다르게 할 수 있었나. **②를 비우면 절반만 답한 것**이고,
실제로 EPTA_01~03 에서 세 세트 연속 비웠던 자리다.

### 뼈대 (6문장)

```
① I think the controller handled it ______ .        ← 주어는 반드시 the controller
② As soon as we ______ , he ______ .
③ He also ______ .
④ One thing that could have been handled differently is that he could have ______ .
⑤ ______ (숫자 근거)
⑥ Other than that, everything he did was good.
```

### ① 첫 문장 — **주어가 관제사다**

```
○  I think the controller handled it well.
○  I think the controller handled it very professionally.

✗  I think we handled it well.            ← 조종사를 평가함 (EPTA_02)
✗  I think it handled well.               ← 주어가 빠져 수동처럼 들림 (SAMPLE_01)
✗  as a pilot, they did the checklist…    ← 조종사부터 평가 (EPTA_05·06)
```

> **네 세트 연속 지적받은 자리.** SAMPLE_01·EPTA_07 에서 해결됐으니 그대로 유지하면 된다.

### ②~③ 관제사가 실제로 한 일

```
As soon as we reported ______ , he ______ .
      gave us holding at ______ so we could work the checklist
      let us descend at our discretion
      gave us priority for the approach
      gave us a vector clear of the high ground

He also ______ .
      gave us two options for the airport and let us choose
      gave us the weather for both fields
      had the fire service in position before we landed
      told us the minimum safe altitude on our track
```

### ④ **가장 중요한 한 문장** — 이 틀을 통째로 외운다

```
One thing that could have been handled differently is that he could have ______ .
```

| 내가 쓴 것 | 고친 것 |
|---|---|
| `one thing that we have been handled differently is` | `One thing that could have been handled differently is` |
| `the one thing that can be handled differently is` | 〃 |
| `one thing that i want to handle different is` | 〃 |

**빈칸 후보** (그동안 실제로 지적할 만했던 것들) :

```
…he could have given us the weather together with the options, instead of after we asked.
…he could have told us about the runway change earlier, while we were still high.
…he could have checked our aircraft type and performance first, and then offered an
   option that was actually suitable for us.
…he could have given us only the one airport that was usable, to save us time.
…he could have relayed our situation to the tower himself, instead of waiting for us
   to request it.
…he could have had the fire service in position earlier.
```

### ⑤ 숫자 근거 — 한 줄만 넣으면 의견이 사실에 묶인다

```
Yangyang was only 2,500 metres when we needed 2,900, so it was never a real choice.
Daleford was 800 metres in fog with no ILS, so it was not usable for us.
We had 70 minutes of fuel and the diversion was 35, so it was possible but thin.
```

> **EPTA_07 Q2 는 지적 내용이 좋았는데 숫자가 하나도 없었다.** `2,500 when we needed 2,900`
> 한 토막이면 "타당한 의견"이 "증명된 의견"이 된다.

### ⑥ 마무리

```
○  Other than that, everything the controller did was good.

✗  other that all the things handled well        ← other than that
✗  all the atc did is perfect                    ← everything the controller did was good
```

## 3. Part Ⅱ A Follow-up — 3부 구조

질문 문구는 세트마다 다르지만 **구조는 늘 셋**이다.

```
① In this situation, ______ ?                 ← 롤플레이에서 왜 그렇게 했나
② In your role as a pilot, ______ ?           ← 일반적으로 어떻게 판단하나
③ Give an example of ______ .                 ← 예시
```

### 뼈대 (7문장)

```
① In this situation, what I needed to know was ______ , because ______ .
   The controller told me ______ , so ______ .
   If ______ had been ______ , I would have ______ .

② In my role as a pilot, I decide this by comparing ______ and ______ .
   ______ always wins, because ______ .

③ For example, I would ______ . ______ costs ______ , but ______ costs ______ .
```

### ① 롤플레이 되짚기

**질문이 "무엇을 알아야 했는가"면 답은 "값"이지 "행동"이 아니다.**

```
○  what I needed to know was the takeoff distance available on runway 32R,
   because our required takeoff distance that day was 2,900 metres.

✗  first ask about change of runway…      ← 행동을 말했다 (EPTA_07)
```

| 알아야 했던 값 | 세트 |
|---|---|
| `the takeoff distance available` | EPTA_07 |
| `the current wind, because my tailwind limit is 10 knots` | EPTA_06 |
| `how many minutes the slowdown would cost us` | EPTA_05 |
| `the weather at both airports` | EPTA_08 |
| `how long the delay would be` | EPTA_01 |

### ② 일반 판단 기준 — `In my role as a pilot, ...`

```
In my role as a pilot, I decide this by comparing two numbers — ______ and ______ .
      how much time it saves          /  my aircraft's limit
      the fuel I have                 /  what the alternate needs
      the distance available          /  the distance I require

______ always wins, because ______ .
      The limit  /  the limit is a performance figure and the schedule is only a schedule
      Fuel       /  you can always make up time, but you cannot make up fuel
```

### ③ `Give an example.` — **여섯 세트 연속 비어 있는 자리**

**경험이 없어도 된다. 가정형으로 답하면 정답이다.**

```
For example, I would ______ .
      turn down a runway change that left me with less than my required distance
      divert straight away if the holding time was longer than my spare fuel
      refuse a short approach when I was high and fast and needed more track miles
      go around rather than accept a late runway change inside five miles

______ costs ______ , but ______ costs ______ .
      Missing a slot costs the company money, but an overrun costs the aircraft.
      Holding costs ten minutes, but arriving below reserve costs you your options.
```

> `I don't have any experience similar like this` 로 닫으면 **질문을 비운 것**이다.
> `I have not had exactly this, but for example, if ______ , I would ______ .` 로 연다.

## 4. 매번 틀리는 문법 — 90초 답변에서만 나오는 것들

| 내 표현 | 고친 표현 | 왜 |
|---|---|---|
| `we notice that warning` | `we noticed that warning` | **과거 사건을 보고하는 것**이므로 과거형 |
| `we request to descend` | `we requested a descent` | 시제 + REQUEST 뒤 명사구 |
| `we get safety situation` | `the cabin altitude stabilized` | 뜻이 전달되지 않음 |
| `we can land the same what we did before` | `we landed using the ILS as we had planned` | |
| `there are a lot of fuel` | `we had too much fuel` | |
| `there are some accidents will happen` | `there was a risk of an accident` | 동사 두 개가 겹침 |
| `it helped us something to find` | `it helped us work out where we could go` | |
| `profit for our aircraft` | `suitable for our aircraft` | `profit`=수익. **두 번 나온 오류** |
| `deroute another taxiway` | `re-route us onto another taxiway` | |
| `to make the safe for our passenger` | `to keep our passengers safe` | |
| `other that` | `other than that` | |

## 5. 90초를 채우는 순서 (실전)

1. **처음 10초는 말하지 않아도 된다.** 메모를 보고 빈칸에 넣을 값을 고른다.
2. **①부터 순서대로 간다.** 중간에 막히면 다음 문장 틀로 넘어간다 — 틀이 있으면 멈추지 않는다.
3. **숫자를 최소 두 개 넣는다.** 고도·거리·시간 중 아무거나. 숫자가 들어가면 Comprehension 과
   Vocabulary 가 같이 올라간다.
4. **`Correction.` 은 감점이 아니다.** 말하다 틀렸으면 그 자리에서 고친다. 실제 구술시험에서
   권장되는 화법이다. (SAMPLE_01 에서 두 번 쓰셨고 둘 다 좋은 판단이었다)
5. **끝에 공허한 문장을 붙이지 않는다.** `all we have to do is just request and do right away`
   같은 마무리는 분량만 차지한다. ③의 예시로 닫는 편이 낫다.

## 부록 — 30초 턴에서 자주 비는 틀

90초는 아니지만 **"마지막 토막"을 여섯 세트 연속 빠뜨린** 자리라 같이 적어 둔다.
지시문에서 `and` 와 쉼표를 세어 **기능 개수를 먼저 적고**, 답에 그만큼 토막이 있는지 본다.

| 지시문에 이 말이 있으면 | 답에 반드시 |
|---|---|
| `with a reason` | `because ______` / `due to ______` 한 토막 |
| `inquire ______` / `tell him what you need to know` | **물음표로 끝나는 문장** |
| `say your intentions` | `Our intention is to ______ .` ← 요청이 아니다 |
| `request assistance` | 사람·장비를 불러 달라는 요청 (`a marshaller`, `the fire service`) |
| `tell him what you need instead` | `Request ______ .` (대안) |
| `say what you will need on arrival` | `Request ______ on arrival.` |
| `two things` | **토막 두 개** |
| `stating you're levelling off at ______` | `We are levelling off at ______ .` ← `maintain` 아님 |
| `advise ______ to stand by` | `Stand by for further information.` |

**숫자를 받았을 때의 고정 동작** (최우선 과제) :

```
받은 값   ______
내 한계   ______      →  부등호를 긋는다  →  말할 때 두 숫자를 모두 입에 올린다

○  3,200 metres is enough — we need 2,900. We will take runway 32R.
○  Yangyang at 2,500 is not enough — we need 2,900. We will continue to Cheongju.
```
