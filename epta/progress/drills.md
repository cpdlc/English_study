# 자주 틀리는 유형 드릴 — 문제 → 정답

9세트 286문항 채점 결과에서 **실패율이 높은 순**으로 모았다. 전부 실제로 푼 문항이고,
정답은 그때 드린 모범답안이다. **`→` 아래를 손으로 가리고 먼저 말해 본 뒤 비교한다.**

| 유형 | 출제 | ✅ | 실패율 | 이 문서 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 90초 자유응답 | 27 | 0 | **100%** | → [`templates-90s.md`](templates-90s.md) |
| **복창 + α** | 16 | 2 | **88%** | **§1** |
| intentions · 두 질문 | 9 | 2 | 78% | §3 · §4 |
| 판단 · 이유 | 35 | 9 | 74% | §2 |
| 발신 (호출·요청·보고) | 69 | 25 | 64% | §5 |
| 복창만 | 85 | 47 | 45% | §6 |

---

## §1. 복창 + α — **가장 많이 틀리는 유형 (88%)**

### 왜 틀리나

복창은 잘한다. **복창을 끝내고 멈춘다.** 지시문이 요구한 두 번째 일을 하지 않는다.

> **복창만 하면 되는 문항은 55% 성공, 복창에 한 가지만 더 붙으면 12% 성공.**
> 복창 능력의 문제가 아니라 **지시문을 끝까지 안 읽는 문제**다.

### 고정 동작

```
지시문에서 and · 쉼표 · then 을 센다  →  기능 개수를 메모에 숫자로 적는다  →
복창을 끝내고 그 숫자만큼 토막이 더 있는지 확인한 뒤 송신을 끝낸다
```

### 지시문 단어 → 반드시 들어갈 토막

| 이 말이 보이면 | 답에 반드시 |
|---|---|
| `and inquire ______` / `tell him what you need to know` | **물음표로 끝나는 문장** |
| `and say your intentions` | `Our intention is to ______ .` |
| `and tell him whether you can do that` | `We can ______ .` 또는 `Negative, we cannot ______ .` |
| `and acknowledge the delay` | `Roger the delay — we will ______ .` |
| `and report what you saw` | `And be advised, ______ .` |
| `stating you're levelling off at ______` | `We are levelling off at ______ .` |
| `and request what you need` | `Request ______ .` |
| `with a reason` | `because ______` / `due to ______` |
| `two things` | **토막 두 개** |

### 드릴

**①** (EPTA_05 P2A-Q2)
```
Center : HL123, descend to flight level 240, expect 10 minutes of holding at TORIN.
☞ Give a full readback, and inquire what that will do to your arrival time.
```
→ `Descend to flight level 240, expect 10 minutes of holding at TORIN, HL123. What will that do to our arrival time? We have a curfew at 2300.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `descent fl240 , expect 10min holding hl123` — 복창에서 멈췄다.*

**②** (EPTA_08 P2A-Q8)
```
Tower : HL123, wind 150 at 12, runway 15, cleared to land. Vacate via Delta if you can,
        the A319 behind you is tight.
☞ Give a full readback, and tell him whether you can do that.
```
→ `Cleared to land runway 15, HL123, and we can vacate via Delta.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `... VR Delta, if able` — 관제사 말을 되돌려준 것이지 답이 아니다.*

**③** (EPTA_08 P2A-Q2)
```
Ground : HL123, taxi to runway 34 via Papa and Golf, hold short of Golf 3. There's an A350
         unserviceable at the holding point ahead of you, so it may be a while.
☞ Give a full readback, and acknowledge the delay.
```
→ `Taxi to runway 34 via Papa and Golf, hold short of Golf 3, HL123. Roger the delay — we will wait for your call.`

**④** (SAMPLE_01 P2B-Q3)
```
Approach : HL123, turn right heading 190, descend and maintain 4,000 to intercept the
           runway 23 localizer.
☞ Respond accordingly, stating you're levelling off at 4,000. Inform them of your
  situation and request vectors for holding.      ← 기능 3개
```
→ `Turn right heading 190, descend and maintain 4,000 to intercept the runway 23 localizer, HL123. We are levelling off at 4,000 — we have lost a lot of our instrument displays. Request vectors for holding.`

**⑤** (EPTA_08 P1B-Q3)
```
(순항 중 10시 방향 2마일에 드론을 봤다. 바빠서 보고하지 못했다.)
Center : HL123, Fenwick Control, radar contact, maintain flight level 330.
☞ Acknowledge the instruction, and report what you saw.
```
→ `Maintain flight level 330, HL123. And be advised, in the cruise we saw what looked like a drone about 2 miles away at our ten o'clock, slightly below.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `Recast label down due to drone` — **보고를 요청으로 바꿨다.** 지나간 목격을
지금의 요청으로 바꾸면 관제사가 현재 위협으로 받는다.*

**⑥** (EPTA_07 P2A-Q9)
```
Departure : HL123, radar contact, climb to flight level 230. I can give you direct SIMUN
            if you want it. Up to you.
☞ Give your full readback, then tell him what you need to know before you accept.
```
→ `Climb to flight level 230, HL123. Before we accept — how many track miles does direct SIMUN save, and what is the weather on that routing?`
*내가 쓴 답 : 묻기만 하고 **복창을 통째로 빠뜨렸다.* 고도는 필수 복창 항목이다.*

## §2. 판단 + 이유 — 출제가 가장 많다 (35문항, 74% 실패)

### 왜 틀리나

**결정은 한다. 이유를 안 댄다.** 또는 이유를 대는데 **숫자가 없다.**

### 뼈대

```
(결정) ______ .  (이유) because / due to ______ .  (요청) Request ______ .
```

### 2-1. 두 숫자를 나란히 말하기 — **최우선, 3세트 연속 실패**

**이 세 문장 중 하나를 반사적으로 꺼낸다.**

```
______ is enough — we need ______ .
______ is inside our limit of ______ .
______ is not enough — we need ______ .
```

**①** 수락이 정답 (EPTA_08 P2B-Q4)
```
(진동 때문에 230노트 이하로 유지해야 한다.)
Approach : HL123, for spacing I need 210 knots or less. Can you do that?
☞ Respond with a reason, and tell him both speeds.
```
→ `Affirm, HL123 — our limit is 230 knots and 210 is inside that, so we can accept 210.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `negative ... we have to use 230 knots or less` — **제한을 정확히 말하면서
그것을 거부 근거로 썼다.** 210은 230보다 느리니 가능하다.*

**②** 수락이 정답 (EPTA_08 P1B-Q1)
```
(필요 이륙거리 2,100 m. Golf 4 교차점 출발은 2,400 m 가용.)
Ground : HL123, are you able to accept an intersection departure from Golf 4?
☞ Respond with a reason, and tell him what distance you need.
```
→ `Affirm, HL123 — we need 2,100 metres and Golf 4 gives us 2,400, so we can accept the intersection departure.`

**③** 거부가 정답 (EPTA_07 P2B-Q5)
```
(필요 착륙거리 2,900 m.)
Approach : HL123, Yangyang has 2,500 metres available. Cheongju has 3,600.
☞ State your decision with a reason.
```
→ `Yangyang at 2,500 metres is not enough — we need 2,900. We will continue to Cheongju with 3,600. Request priority for landing.`

**④** 수락이 정답 (EPTA_07 P2A-Q4) — **틀렸던 문항**
```
(필요 이륙거리 2,900 m.)
Ground : HL123, runway 32R has 3,200 metres available, and the surface is dry.
☞ State your decision with a reason.
```
→ `3,200 metres is enough — we need 2,900. We will take runway 32R.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `we cannot use the 32R due to aircraft performance we need at least 2900m`
— **부등호가 뒤집혔다.** 3,200 > 2,900 이므로 쓸 수 있다.*

**⑤** 시간 계산 (EPTA_08 P2A-Q4)
```
(연료 1시간 5분. 제설 15분.)
Approach : HL123, once they're done it'll be about 10 more minutes to touchdown.
☞ State your decision with a reason, and tell him how much fuel you have.
```
→ `We will take the hold, HL123 — 15 minutes plus 10 to touchdown is 25 minutes, and we have 1 hour 5 minutes of fuel, so that is well inside our reserve.`

**⑥** 뺄셈이 필요 (EPTA_08 P1B-Q6)
```
(WYNTA 홀딩 중. 연료 1시간 10분. 대체공항은 여기서 25분. EFC 20분을 방금 받았다.)
☞ Contact Marchfield Approach. Tell him whether you can accept that, and say what your limit is.
```
→ `Marchfield Approach, HL123 holding at WYNTA — we can accept the 20-minute delay. We have 1 hour 10 minutes of fuel and our alternate needs 25, so we can hold for up to 45 minutes.`

**⑦** 못 하는 것 (EPTA_05 P1B-Q1)
```
(FL240, TORIN 까지 40마일 남음. 강하에 43마일 필요.)
Center : HL123, cross TORIN at 11,000 feet.
☞ Respond with a reason, and tell him what you need.
```
→ `Unable to cross TORIN at 11,000, HL123 — we have 40 miles to run and we need 43 for that descent. Request descent now, or a speed reduction.`

### 2-2. 한계 때문에 못 할 때 — **unable 3단**

```
Unable (못 하는 것) due to (사유). Request (대안).
```

**①** (EPTA_08 P1B-Q4)
```
(RNAV 장비가 MEL 로 비작동.)
Approach : HL123, expect the RNAV runway 15 approach.
☞ Respond with a reason, and tell him what you need instead.
```
→ `Unable the RNAV runway 15 approach, HL123 — our RNAV is inoperative under the minimum equipment list. Request the ILS runway 15.`

**②** (EPTA_06 P1B-Q4)
```
(12마일 남았는데 강하·형상 전환에 18마일이 필요하다.)
Approach : HL123, are you able to accept a short approach to runway 33?
☞ Respond with a reason, and tell him what you need instead.
```
→ `Negative, HL123 — we are high and fast and we need about 18 miles of track. Request vectors for a longer final.`

**③** (EPTA_05 P1B-Q4)
```
(어두운 밤, 수평선이 없고 동쪽 지형이 생소하다.)
Approach : HL123, are you able to accept a visual approach?
```
→ `Negative, HL123 — it is a dark night with no horizon and we are not familiar with the terrain east of the field. Request vectors for the ILS.`

### 2-3. ATC 가 대안을 먼저 준 경우 — **요청이 아니라 수락**

> `if unable, (대안)` 을 관제사가 **먼저** 말했으면 그 대안을 **요청하지 말고 받는다.**
> SAMPLE_01 과 EPTA_07 에서 두 번 틀린 유형.

**①** (EPTA_07 P1B-Q5)
```
(현재 중량으로 FL380 에 도달할 수 없다.)
Center : HL123, climb to flight level 380. If unable, maintain flight level 340 until SIMUN.
```
→ `Unable flight level 380 due to weight, HL123. Maintaining flight level 340 until SIMUN.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `climb to fl380 , maintain fl340 until simun` — **두 고도를 동시에 복창해
모순된 교신**이 됐다. `unable` 선언이 먼저다.*

**②** (SAMPLE_01 P1B-Q4)
```
Center : HL123, climb and maintain flight level 210. Cross Johnson VOR at or above flight
         level 150. If unable, maintain flight level 130 until Johnson VOR.
```
→ `Climbing to flight level 210, HL123. Unable to cross Johnson VOR at or above flight level 150 due to performance — we will maintain flight level 130 until Johnson VOR.`

## §3. intentions — `Say your intentions` (여섯 세트에서 지적)

### 왜 틀리나

**요청을 intentions 로 착각한다.** `request priority for landing` 은 요청이지 의도가 아니다.

### 뼈대

```
Our intention is to ______ , and then ______ .
          (지금 할 일)        (그다음 할 일 / 필요한 지원)
```

**①** (EPTA_08 P2B-Q5)
```
Approach : HL123, turn left heading 300 for vectors to the ILS, descend to 6,000 feet.
           Say your intentions.
☞ Give a full readback and say your intentions.
```
→ `Turn left heading 300 for vectors to the ILS, descend to 6,000 feet, HL123. Our intention is to continue for the ILS runway 27 and land at Oakport with the fire service standing by.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `Our intention is to land as soon as possible` — 구문은 맞는데 **관제사가
준비할 것이 없다.** 어느 접근으로, 어디에, 무엇이 필요한지가 들어가야 한다.*

**②** (EPTA_07 P2B-Q6)
```
Approach : HL123, turn right heading 030 for vectors to the ILS, descend to 5,000 feet.
           Say your intentions.
```
→ `Turn right heading 030 for vectors to the ILS, descend to 5,000 feet, HL123. Our intention is to land at Cheongju off the ILS runway 34, with the fire service standing by.`

**③** (EPTA_05 P2B-Q4)
```
Approach : HL123, hold at VESPA, left turns, maintain 8,000 feet. Say your intentions
           when you are ready.
```
→ `Holding at VESPA, left turns, maintain 8,000 feet, HL123. Our intention is to work the checklist, then request a low pass for a visual check of the nose gear before we commit to landing.`

**④** (EPTA_06 P2B-Q5)
```
Center : HL123, turn right heading 290, that will take you clear of the high ground in
         15 miles, descend to 10,000 feet. Say your intentions.
```
→ `Turn right heading 290, descend to 10,000 feet, HL123. Our intention is to level at 10,000, complete the checklist, and then continue to Westmoor for the ILS.`

## §4. 두 질문 — `two things`

### 왜 틀리나

**관제사가 둘이라고 세어 줬는데 하나만 답한다.** EPTA_04·05 에서 지적 → 06·07 해결 →
08 재발.

### 뼈대

```
(첫 질문 답) ______ , and (둘째 질문 답) ______ .
```

**①** (EPTA_08 P2B-Q6)
```
Approach : HL123, two things — do you want the fire service, and will you need to shut
           that engine down before landing?
```
→ `HL123, affirm, we would like the fire service standing by as a precaution. And negative, we do not need to shut the engine down — it is still running at reduced thrust.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `I think the fire Services because there's no fire in the engine` — 불이
없는데 소방대를 원한다는 **모순**이 됐고, 둘째 질문은 답하지 않았다. 이유는
**`as a precaution`** 이다.*

**②** (EPTA_07 P2B-Q7)
```
Approach : HL123, two things — say your fuel endurance and the number of persons on board.
(연료 1시간 25분, 승객 172명 + 승무원 8명)
```
→ `HL123, fuel endurance 1 hour 25 minutes, and 180 persons on board.`

**③** (EPTA_05 P2B-Q9)
```
Approach : HL123, the tower is asking two things — will you be stopping on the runway,
           and do you want the fire service alongside or following you?
```
→ `HL123, we do not expect to stop on the runway, and we would like the fire service following us.`

**④** (EPTA_06 P2B-Q6)
```
Center : HL123, two things — say your present rate of descent, and tell me whether anyone
         on board needs medical attention.
(강하율 4,000 ft/min, 승객 2명이 불편하지만 긴급 치료는 불필요)
```
→ `HL123, our rate of descent is about 4,000 feet per minute, and two passengers are feeling unwell but nobody needs urgent treatment.`
*`altitude`(고도)와 `rate of descent`(강하율)는 다른 질문이다.*

## §5. 발신 문항 — 내가 먼저 부를 때 (69문항, 64% 실패)

### 왜 틀리나

호출은 하는데 **지문이 준 숫자를 빠뜨린다.** 또는 **이미 받은 것을 다시 요청한다.**

### 5-1. 첫 두 단어를 고정한다

```
(지시문이 알려준 기관명) , HL123 , ______
```

지시문에 `Contact Apron` 이라고 적혀 있으면 **`Apron`** 이지 `Ground` 가 아니다.
SAMPLE_01 에서 두 번 틀렸다(Clearance→Ground, Apron→Ground).

### 5-2. 위치보고 — 고정형

```
(기관) , HL123 , passing ______ for ______ , ______ .
                        지금 고도    목표 고도   (거리·ATIS·요청)
```

**①** (EPTA_08 P2A-Q1)
```
(FL290, DORLIN 까지 90마일, 연료 1시간 5분)
☞ Contact Fenwick Control, report your position and request descent.
```
→ `Fenwick Control, HL123, level at flight level 290, 90 miles from DORLIN, request descent.`
*내가 쓴 답 : `Miles from dolin` — **숫자 90 이 사라졌다.** 거리 없는 "~마일"은 정보가
아니다. 그리고 `request inbound` 는 요청이 아니다.*

**②** (EPTA_06 P2A-Q9)
```
(이륙 후 1,800 피트 통과, 3,000 피트로 상승 중)
☞ Contact Arlen Departure, report your passing altitude and request further climb.
```
→ `Arlen Departure, HL123, passing 1,800 feet for 3,000, request further climb.`
*`climb to 3,000` 은 **지시를 복창하는 동사**다. 보고에는 `passing X for Y` 를 쓴다.*

**③** (EPTA_08 P2A-Q7)
```
(ILS 15 에 안착, 2,200 피트 통과)
☞ Contact Marchfield Tower, report your position and intention.
```
→ `Marchfield Tower, HL123, established on the ILS runway 15, passing 2,200 feet, request landing.`

**④** (EPTA_05 P2A-Q4)
```
(FL180 통과, 11,000 피트로 강하 중)
☞ Contact Larkin Approach and report your passing altitude.
```
→ `Larkin Approach, HL123, descending through flight level 180 for 11,000 feet.`

### 5-3. REQUEST 뒤에는 **명사구**

| ✗ | ○ |
|---|---|
| `request to take off` | `request priority for departure` |
| `request increase speed` | `request higher speed` |
| `request decrease speed` | `request vectors for a longer final` |
| `request climb to 2 levels above` | `request two levels higher` |
| `request for departure` | `request priority for departure` |
| `request to direct Lakeside` | `request direct Lakeside` |

### 5-4. 이미 받은 것은 요청하지 않는다

> 지문이나 관제사가 **이미 말했으면 그걸 쓰고**, 모르는 것만 요청한다.

```
✗  Request expected further clearance.     ← EFC 20분을 방금 받은 상태 (EPTA_08 P1B-Q6)
✗  Request hold due to 30 minutes hold.    ← 30분 홀드를 통보받은 직후 (SAMPLE_01 P2A-Q9)
✗  Request climb to FL130 until Johnson.   ← ATC 가 이미 준 대안 (SAMPLE_01 P1B-Q4)
```

### 5-5. 사건을 알릴 때 — 이름 + 조치 + 요청

```
(기관) , HL123 , (사건 이름) . We have (조치) . Request (필요한 것) .
```

**①** (EPTA_08 P2B-Q3)
```
(15,000 피트 통과 중 조류 떼 통과. 엔진 2 심한 진동. 추력을 줄여 진동이 가라앉음.
 230노트 이하 유지. 화재 없고 엔진은 계속 운전 중.)
☞ Inform the controller, tell him what you have done about it, and request priority for landing.
```
→ `Approach, HL123, passing 15,000 feet we flew through a flock of birds and engine number 2 is vibrating. We have reduced thrust on that engine and the vibration has settled. Request priority for landing.`

**②** (EPTA_07 P2B-Q3) — MAYDAY
```
(후방 화물칸 CARGO SMOKE 경고. 소화병 방출. 소화제 60분.)
☞ Declare an emergency, inform the controller of your situation, and request priority for landing.
```
→ `Mayday, mayday, mayday, Cheongju Approach, HL123, cargo smoke warning in the aft hold. We have discharged the fire bottles. Request priority for landing at Cheongju.`
*선언 순서는 **MAYDAY×3 → 기관명 → 콜사인 → 사건**.*

**③** (EPTA_06 P2B-Q3)
```
(CABIN ALTITUDE 경고, 객실고도 10,000 통과 상승, 마스크 전개.)
☞ Inform the controller, tell him you are starting an emergency descent, and request a lower level.
```
→ `Eastvale Control, HL123, we have a cabin altitude warning and we are losing pressurization. We are starting an emergency descent now. Request descent to 10,000 feet.`

### 5-6. 갱신 — **"지금 어떤가"** 를 먼저

```
✗  We have some abnormal issues about engine before.   ← 전에 있었다 (EPTA_08)
✗  We got discharge on the display.                    ← 이미 한 일 (EPTA_07)
○  The vibration has dropped and engine number 2 is now running normally.
○  The smoke warning went out about three minutes after we discharged the bottles.
```

## §6. 복창만 — 그래도 남는 실수

55% 성공으로 가장 잘하는 유형이지만, 아래 넷은 계속 나온다.

### 6-1. `cleared` 계열 — 한 단어가 허가를 바꾼다

| ✗ | ○ | 차이 |
|---|---|---|
| `clear direct ATLAS` | **`cleared`** direct ATLAS | 다섯 세트째 반복 |
| `cleared to runway 32R` | **`cleared for takeoff`** runway 32R | "활주로까지 가도 좋다" vs "이륙 허가" |
| `cleared to take off` | **`cleared for takeoff`** | `takeoff` 는 이륙 허가에만 쓰는 한 단어 |
| `expect the ILS runway 36` | **`cleared for`** the ILS runway 36 approach | 예상 vs 허가 |
| `line up and wait` ← `cleared for takeoff` | 둘을 바꿔 말하면 **허가 없는 이륙** | |

### 6-2. 조건절도 복창 대상

```
○  Expedite climb **until passing** flight level 180, then climb to flight level 310.
○  **After departure** climb to flight level 330, report passing 5,000 feet.
○  Descend to 3,000 feet **until established**, cleared for the ILS runway 15 approach.
○  **Behind the departing Cessna Citation**, line up and wait runway 32R.
○  Cross WYNTA **at 220 knots or less**.
```
*조건을 빼면 "지금 바로"가 된다. `behind the ~` 누락은 활주로 침입으로 직결된다.*

### 6-3. 복창하지 않는 것 — 빼는 것도 정확도다

```
caution wake turbulence / caution jet blast / caution birds near the threshold
winds 220 at 10  (착륙·이륙 허가에 붙은 바람)
traffic crossing right to left / traffic landing
due to traffic / for spacing      ← 사유는 복창 불필요
```

### 6-4. 숫자 — 지금 남은 두 곳

```
만 단위 고도에서 0 이 빠진다        10,000 → `1000`     15,000 → `1,500`
     → one zero thousand / one five thousand 처럼 자릿수를 끊어 읽는다

발신할 때 거리 숫자가 통째로 빠진다   `90 miles from DORLIN` → `miles from dolin`
     → Initial Situation 의 숫자를 메모 맨 위에 적어 두고 그대로 읽는다
```

**네 자리 피트(2,200 · 5,000 · 1,800)는 이제 정확하다.** 다섯 자리만 남았다.

## §7. 20초 Ⅰ-B 의 세 고정 패턴

### 7-1. `Confirm maintaining ______ ?` — **함정형, 매 세트 1문항**

```
Negative , (실제 상태) , (사유) .
```

| 상황 | 정답 |
|---|---|
| 2,500 지시, 지형경고로 3,000 에 있음 | `Negative, maintaining 3,000 feet due to terrain caution.` |
| 310 지시, 뇌우 회피로 290 에 있음 | `Negative, maintaining heading 290 to stay clear of a build-up.` |
| 7,000 지시, **관제사가 5,000 으로 내려줬음** | `Negative, level at 5,000 feet — you cleared us down to 5,000 a few minutes ago.` |
| 10도 우선회 지시, 실수로 좌선회함 | `Negative — we turned 10 degrees left by mistake. Now turning right 10 degrees.` |

> **첫 단어는 언제나 `Negative`.** 설명부터 시작하면 관제사가 끝까지 듣고 나서야 판단한다.
> `are you able ~?` 도 같다 — `I'm not sure` 는 표준 응답이 아니다.
> 긍정이면 **`Affirm`**(`Affirmative` 아님).

### 7-2. 정정 3단 — 순서까지

```
(관제사가 물은 값을 먼저) . Correction — we had (틀린 값) set by mistake. Now (조치) .
```

| 상황 | 정답 |
|---|---|
| squawk 6170 을 6107 로 입력 | `We had 6107 set by mistake. Now squawking 6170.` |
| FL200 허가를 FL220 으로 세팅 | `We were cleared to flight level 200. Correction — we had 220 set in the altitude window by mistake. Now descending back to 200.` |
| 128.3 을 128.5 로 세팅 | `We were given 128.3. Correction — we had 128.5 set by mistake. Now going to 128.3.` |

> **틀린 값을 반드시 말한다.** `we set another squawk by mistake` 로는 관제사가 화면과
> 대조할 수 없다. 그리고 `altimeter`(고도계 기압)와 `altitude window`(고도 선택창)는 다르다.

### 7-3. 시차형 보고 — 나중에 알리기

```
(복창) . And be advised , (언제) we saw (무엇) (어디서) .
```

| 상황 | 정답 |
|---|---|
| 이륙 활주 중 활주로 끝 조류 | `And be advised, during our takeoff roll we saw bird activity at the end of the runway.` |
| 최종접근 중 좌측 진입등 4개 소등 | `And be advised, on final we noticed about four approach lights out on the left side.` |
| 이륙 중 Juliet 에서 정비차량 진입 | `And be advised, during our takeoff roll we saw a maintenance vehicle moving towards the runway from taxiway Juliet.` |
| 순항 중 10시 2마일 드론 | `And be advised, in the cruise we saw what looked like a drone about 2 miles away at our ten o'clock.` |

> **시점을 먼저 밝힌다.** `is going` 으로 말하면 지금 들어가는 중이라는 뜻이 되어
> 관제사가 활주로를 닫는다.
