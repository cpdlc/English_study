--- 프롬프트 (ChatGPT / Gemini 등에 그대로 붙여넣기) ---

Create a flat, top-down airport ground chart illustration in the style of an aviation
English test diagram. Requirements:

- Black-and-white satellite-style airport layout: dark grey/black terminal buildings,
  mid-grey taxiways, darker grey runways with white dashed centrelines. No colour in the
  background at all.
- Two runways: one on the LEFT labelled "RWY 34", one on the RIGHT labelled "RWY 32R",
  both running roughly top-to-bottom.
- Taxiway letters in black bold: "P" and "G" leading to RWY 34 (with a holding point
  marked "G3"), and "H" leading to RWY 32R.
- Two aircraft drawn as simple LIGHT BLUE silhouettes seen from above (no other colour
  on the aircraft).
- The aircraft near the G3 holding point has a white rectangular label box with a thin
  black border reading "A350 U/S", connected to it by a RED arrow, and that aircraft is
  circled with a RED circle.
- The second aircraft, further back on taxiway G, has a white rectangular label box
  reading "HL123" connected by a RED arrow.
- In the upper-right empty area, bold RED text on two lines:
  "RWY 32R ??" and "Distance available ??"
- No other text, no legend, no compass rose, no watermark, no photo-realistic detail.
- Clean, high contrast, readable at a glance in 15 seconds. Landscape, about 680x400.
