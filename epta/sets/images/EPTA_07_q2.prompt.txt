--- 프롬프트 ---

Create a clean, flat illustration of an airliner overhead FIRE PROTECTION panel as it
would appear in an aviation English test. Requirements:

- Dark cockpit panel background, viewed straight on.
- A large illuminated warning light in RED reading "CARGO SMOKE", with a smaller
  caption under it reading "AFT HOLD".
- Next to it, an illuminated amber/white indicator reading "BOTTLES DISCHARGED".
- Below, three simple rectangular readout boxes with labels and values:
  "SUPPRESSANT REMAINING  60 MIN", "CABIN  CLEAR" (in green),
  "LDG DIST REQD  2,900 m".
- At the bottom, bold RED text: "Nearest suitable field ??"
- No switches, no extra knobs, no clutter, no watermark, no brand names.
- High contrast, large labels, readable at a glance in 15 seconds. Landscape, 660x330.
