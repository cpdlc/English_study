**Dunmore Logistics Opens Automated Sorting Facility**

Dunmore Logistics unveiled its new automated sorting facility last week, a $12 million investment the company says will nearly double its regional package-handling capacity. The 200,000-square-foot facility, located just outside Hartwell, uses conveyor-based scanning technology to sort packages by destination without manual handling for most of the process.

Company president Renata Ibarra said the facility was built in response to a sharp increase in delivery volume over the past two years, driven largely by growth in online retail. Ibarra noted that the company viewed retraining as preferable to reducing headcount, citing the value of experienced staff familiar with the company's operations.

Dunmore Logistics plans to apply lessons learned from the Hartwell facility to two additional automation projects planned for next year.
